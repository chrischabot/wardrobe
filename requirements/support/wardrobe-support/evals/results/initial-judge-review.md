The review distinguishes several material patterns: recognizing the academic register does not excuse flattery; variety does not require rigid shoe quotas; a scoped swap must preserve the rest of the outfit; and missing waist measurements cannot be replaced with chest arithmetic. The charcoal-trouser comparison is a defensible recommendation even though another well-supported combination could also pass.

One image-dependent reply remains insufficiently evidenced. The judge does not fabricate the image, declare the old assistant wrong merely because its attachment is missing, or treat its confident prose as verification.
