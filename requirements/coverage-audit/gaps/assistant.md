# Coverage gaps: assistant (conversation authority, memory, research, lifecycle, inference)

Audited at `garderobe-rebuild` commit `8445a1e`, by reading code and tests; nothing was executed. "spec" is `requirements/garderobe-replacement-design.md`. Paths beginning `src/` or `test/` are under `packages/assistant/`. Per-row verdicts are in `../rows/`; the part letter after each entry says which file.

What holds: no direct SQL write to wardrobe tables exists in `packages/assistant/src`; turn changes go through the command service (`src/tools/runtime.ts:290`) with a ledger-side backstop tested at `test/confirmation.test.ts:114`. Every conversation, composition, vision and research test uses a labelled fake model; no live model has produced a reply.

The largest finding in this area is a policy question for the owner, not a code defect: in conversation only wear and wash reports are recorded at once and every other change waits for the owner's confirmation in the app (rows S06-009, S06-030, S06-032, S10-050, S10-085, S13-036). It is set out under "Owner questions" in `../../COVERAGE.md`.
